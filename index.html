Katherine Dunlap

GRAPHIC DESIGNER

I am a graphic designer who works primarily in Adobe InDesign, Photoshop, and Illustrator. I am currently attending Red Rocks Community College and studying Graphic Design.

As a designer I pride myself on being professional, creative, attentive, adaptive, and detailed. These are not just characteristics, rather they are the pillars I bring to every project. You can expect my absolute best when attending to your design needs.

Take some time to view my design galleries that highlight my work in branding, print design, and web layouts. You will see that I draw inspiration from minimalist and clean designs with a focus on accessible color palettes. My work uses simple imagery and balanced compositions to evoke a calm and structured design.

I look forward to the opportunity to leverage my skills to help you design a website, create print materials, or develop your brand identity. Feel free to contact me about your design needs.
